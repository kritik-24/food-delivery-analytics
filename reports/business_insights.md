# Food Delivery Analytics — Business Insights

## 1. Project Objective
Analyze food delivery operations to understand order cancellations, delivery delays, restaurant performance, and daily order trends using Power BI.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators
- Total Orders: Approximately 5,000
- Cancelled Orders: 634
- Cancellation Rate: 12.68%
- Overall Delay Rate: 68.46%
- Average Delivery Time: 60.63 minutes

*These figures are from the current dashboard and should be validated against the underlying data before final publication.*

## 3. Initial Observations
- Order cancellations represent 12.68% of all orders.
- Delivery partner unavailability is the largest displayed cancellation reason.
- The restaurant-level delayed-order chart shows differences in delayed-order counts.
- Daily order volume fluctuates over the displayed period.
- The preparation-time versus delivery-time chart requires further investigation before drawing conclusions about the causes of delays.

## 4. Recommended Next Steps
1. Investigate delivery partner availability and assignment failures.
2. Compare cancellation rates across restaurants and cities.
3. Examine delivery time by distance, preparation time, and delay status.
4. Validate delay-rate calculations and check for missing or inconsistent records.
5. Test operational improvements and measure their impact before recommending a rollout.

## 5. Tools Used
Power BI, SQL, Python, Pandas, and Git/GitHub.

## 6. Conclusion
The dashboard provides an initial view of food delivery performance and identifies areas for further root-cause analysis. Additional validation and targeted experiments are needed to establish causes and measure the impact of potential improvements.
